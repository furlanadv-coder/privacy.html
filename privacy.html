01. Who we are and scope

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications published under the OnlyAPPle brand by Fabiano Carneiro Furlan, based in Brazil (referred to as “we,” “us,” or “our”). It describes general practices and app-specific information below.

Different applications may use different features and services. Where an application has additional privacy disclosures, those disclosures supplement this policy. This policy does not imply that every listed category of data is collected by every application.

02. Information that may be processed

Depending on the app and features you choose to use, information may include:

  • App settings: preferences, selected options, and saved configurations.
  • Device inputs: camera, microphone, motion, location, or sensor information, only where the app needs those inputs and applicable permissions are granted.
  • Support communications: information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.
  • Technical information: limited diagnostics or operational information, if a particular app or enabled service actually collects it.

Processing information on your device is not necessarily the same as collecting it on our servers. App-specific disclosures below explain the distinction where relevant.

03. Why information is used

Where applicable, information is processed to provide the features you request, remember your preferences, respond to support inquiries, maintain reliability and security, and comply with applicable legal obligations. The relevant legal basis depends on the specific activity and applicable law, and may include performance of a requested service, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligations.

04. Device permissions and local processing

Some applications require access to operating-system features such as alarms, notifications, camera, microphone, photos, Bluetooth, or location. Access depends on the particular app and the permissions available through your device settings. Permissions can generally be reviewed or withdrawn in iOS Settings, although doing so may disable related features.

Whenever practical, we design features to process information locally. Local settings and files may remain on your device until you remove them, reset the app, or uninstall it, subject to the operating system’s backup and synchronization behavior.

05. Service providers and third parties

Applications may rely on operating-system services provided by Apple and, in future versions, other service providers. If an app introduces analytics, advertising, cloud processing, artificial intelligence services, or other third-party integrations that affect personal information, we will update the relevant app-specific disclosure and applicable store privacy declarations.

We do not sell personal information as part of the CALM WAKE functionality described below. This statement is not a representation about future applications or features that have not yet been released.

06. Data retention

Data stored only on your device is generally managed through the app and your device settings. If you contact us, we may retain your correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, keep appropriate records, or meet legal obligations. Where other services are introduced, their retention practices will be described as applicable.

07. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, including Brazil’s LGPD and the EU/EEA GDPR, you may have rights to request information about processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. Rights are subject to legal requirements and exceptions.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. We may request limited information necessary to verify and process your request.

08. International data transfers

Where an app uses services operating outside your country, personal information may be processed in other jurisdictions. When required, appropriate safeguards will be applied under applicable law. The current CALM WAKE features described below do not require an OnlyAPPle-operated cloud account.

09. Children and age-appropriate use

Our applications may have different age ratings and intended audiences. We do not assume that all OnlyAPPle applications are intended for children. Any application specifically directed at children will require a separate assessment and additional disclosures and safeguards where necessary.

10. Security

We seek to use safeguards appropriate to the information and functionality involved. No device, network, or system can be guaranteed completely secure. You can help protect your information by keeping your device and operating system updated.

11. App-specific privacy details

iOS · CALM WAKE

CALM WAKE — Alarm & Pure Frequencies

CALM WAKE lets you configure alarm times, recurrence, sound frequency, intensity, and progressive preview settings. The app uses iOS alarm and audio functionality.

  • Account: no account or sign-in is required.
  • App preferences: alarm and audio settings are intended to be stored locally on your device.
  • Permissions: alarm authorization may be requested to schedule system alarms.
  • Audio: pure-frequency preview audio is generated on the device. The app does not need microphone access for this feature.
  • Advertising and tracking: the current implementation is not designed to show third-party ads or track users across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Developer-operated server: core alarm and preview functionality does not require a developer-operated account or cloud service.

These details describe the app implementation reviewed for this policy. They must be checked against the exact release build and updated if functionality, SDKs, or data practices change. App Store privacy labels are declared separately for each app.

Additional OnlyAPPle applications will be added to this section as they are released and their data practices are verified.
